Viva-ready explanation

Why the algorithms are included.
Quick Sort: comparison-based sorting with average O(n log n).
Radix Sort: non-comparison integer sorting with O(d·n).
Binary Search: logarithmic lookup after ordering the IDs.
Sentinel Search: linear lookup using a sentinel to simplify the loop condition.
